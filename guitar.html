🎸 Quick Guide

A fast tour of what each part of the Guitar page does.

Chord Library

Pick a root and chord type to see fingerings, then tap a voicing to view it on the fretboard.

Capo Explorer

Slide the capo to a fret and choose a shape to hear and see the sounding chord.

Scale Explorer

Browse scales across the whole neck, with degrees marked on every note.

CAGED System

Learn the 5 CAGED shapes and how they connect up the neck.

Effects Pedals

In Chord Library and Capo Explorer, tap a pedal to open its controls and the footswitch to turn it on. Amp swaps in an electric guitar tone; Overdrive needs Amp on first.
