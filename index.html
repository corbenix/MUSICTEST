🎶 Quick Start Guide

Chord Nexus in a nutshell — what's in each section, and where to go for what.

🎸
Guitar

Chord Library, Capo Explorer, Scale Explorer, and the CAGED system, plus effects pedals for chord and capo tones.

Bass

Full fretboard scale positions and root-note maps for 4- and 5-string bass.

🎹
Keyboard

An interactive piano with a Scale Explorer, Chord Finder, and chord inversions.

🎵
Chord Builder

Build and hear chord progressions in any key, or browse common progressions by key.

🧰
Other Tools

A chromatic tuner, metronome, and a song key analyzer that detects key and chords from an audio file.
